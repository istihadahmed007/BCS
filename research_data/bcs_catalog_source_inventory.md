## 3. Copyright & Legal Rights Policy

1. **Catalog Nature:** The catalog is an index of third-party user uploads on Google Drive. The catalog disclaims ownership and notes that files may not be cleared for commercial redistribution.
2. **Prohibition of Verbatim Copying:**
   - Reproducing full PDF scans, commercial book layouts, or pirated copyrighted text violates the Bangladesh Copyright Act (Act No. XXVIII of 2000, amended 2005) and international Berne Convention protections.
   - Specifically, works by living authors or copyrighted publications (e.g. *Wren & Martin*, *Nova's GRE Math Bible*, commercial BCS guidebooks) must **never** be copied verbatim.
3. **Lawful Educational Use (Fair Dealing):**
   - Facts, historical dates, grammatical rules, mathematical theorems, scientific formulas, and constitutional articles are not copyrightable.
   - Every question in the BCS question bank must be an **original editorial creation** (`original_from_reference`), synthesizing factual principles into newly authored stems, clean mutually exclusive distractors, and original pedagogical explanations.
4. **Attribution & Provenance:**
   - Record the verified factual source title and topic reference.
   - All time-sensitive current affairs must include an explicit "as of" verification date.
