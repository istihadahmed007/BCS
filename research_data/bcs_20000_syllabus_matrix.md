## 2. Granular Syllabus Topic Mapping & Subtopic Blueprint

### SUB-01: Bangla Language & Literature (3,500 Questions)
1. **Bangla Grammar & Linguistics (1,500 Qs / 15 Marks):**
   - Sound & Letters (ধ্বনি ও বর্ণ): উচ্চারণ স্থান, ঘোষ-অঘোষ, মহাপ্রাণ-অল্পপ্রাণ (150 Qs)
   - Euphonic Combination (সন্ধি): স্বরসন্ধি, ব্যঞ্জনসন্ধি, বিসর্গসন্ধি, নিপাতনে সিদ্ধ (200 Qs)
   - Natva & Satva Vidhan (ণ-ত্ব ও ষ-ত্ব বিধান): নিয়মাবলী ও ব্যতিক্রম (150 Qs)
   - Compound Words (সমাস): দ্বন্দ্ব, কর্মধারয়, তৎপুরুষ, বহুব্রীহি, দ্বিগু, অব্যয়ীভাব (250 Qs)
   - Case & Inflection (কারক ও বিভক্তি): সম্বন্ধ পদ, বিভিন্ন কারকে বিভিন্ন বিভক্তি (200 Qs)
   - Prefixes & Suffixes (উপসর্গ ও প্রত্যয়): খাঁটি বাংলা, তৎসম ও বিদেশী উপসর্গ; কৃৎ ও তদ্ধিত প্রত্যয় (200 Qs)
   - Vocabulary, Semantics & Orthography (শব্দার্থ, বাক্য শুদ্ধি, বিপরীত ও সমার্থক শব্দ, বাগধারা) (350 Qs)
2. **Ancient & Medieval Bangla Literature (500 Qs / 5 Marks):**
   - Charyapada (চর্যাপদ): আবিষ্কার, পদকর্তা, ধর্মমত, ভাষা ও ছন্দ (200 Qs)
   - Medieval Literature (শ্রীকৃষ্ণকীর্তন, বৈষ্ণব পদাবলী, মঙ্গলকাব্য, অনুবাদ সাহিত্য, প্রণয়োপাখ্যান, মৈমনসিংহ গীতিকা) (300 Qs)
3. **Modern Bangla Literature (1,500 Qs / 15 Marks):**
   - 19th Century Renaissance (ঈশ্বরচন্দ্র বিদ্যাসাগর, মাইকেল মধুসূদন, বঙ্কিমচন্দ্র, মীর মশাররফ হোসেন) (300 Qs)
   - Rabindranath Tagore (রবীন্দ্র সাহিত্য ও নোবেল পুরস্কার) (300 Qs)
   - Kazi Nazrul Islam (নজরুল জীবন ও সাহিত্য) (250 Qs)
   - Major 20th Century Literary Figures (জীবনানন্দ দাশ, জসীমউদ্দীন, তারাশঙ্কর, মানিক, বিভূতিভূষণ) (250 Qs)
   - Post-1947 & Liberation War Literature (শামসুর রাহমান, হাসান আজিজুল হক, হুমায়ূন আহমেদ, সৈয়দ হক, আখতারুজ্জামান ইলিয়াস) (400 Qs)

### SUB-02: English Language & Literature (3,500 Questions)
1. **English Language & Grammar (2,000 Qs / 20 Marks):**
   - Parts of Speech: Noun, Pronoun, Adjective, Verb, Adverb, Preposition, Conjunction, Interjection (400 Qs)
   - Idioms, Phrases & Appropriate Prepositions (400 Qs)
   - Clauses & Sentence Structure: Simple, Complex, Compound; Conditionals (250 Qs)
   - Subject-Verb Agreement & Inversion (250 Qs)
   - Transformation of Sentences, Voice & Narration (250 Qs)
   - Vocabulary & Lexical Skills: Synonyms, Antonyms, One-Word Substitution, Word Formation (450 Qs)
2. **English Literature (1,500 Qs / 15 Marks):**
   - Old & Middle English Period (Beowulf, Chaucer) (100 Qs)
   - Renaissance & Elizabethan Age (Shakespeare, Christopher Marlowe, Spenser, Bacon) (350 Qs)
   - Neoclassical & Enlightenment (Milton, Dryden, Alexander Pope, Swift) (200 Qs)
   - Romantic Period (Wordsworth, Coleridge, Byron, Shelley, Keats, Jane Austen) (350 Qs)
   - Victorian Period (Tennyson, Browning, Charles Dickens, George Eliot, Hardy) (250 Qs)
   - Modern & Post-Modern Literature (T.S. Eliot, W.B. Yeats, George Orwell, G.B. Shaw, Hemingway) (250 Qs)

### SUB-03: Bangladesh Affairs (3,000 Questions)
1. **History, Language Movement & Liberation War (1,000 Qs / 10 Marks):**
   - Ancient & Medieval Bengal (Maurya, Gupta, Pala, Sena, Sultanate, Mughal) (200 Qs)
   - Colonial Rule & Anti-British Movements (1757–1947) (200 Qs)
   - Pakistan Period & Language Movement (1947–1952) (150 Qs)
   - Road to Autonomy: 6-Point Movement, 1969 Mass Uprising, 1970 Election (150 Qs)
   - 1971 Liberation War: Declaration, Mujibnagar Government, Sectors, Bir Sreshthos, Victory (300 Qs)
2. **Constitution of Bangladesh, Governance & Public Administration (800 Qs / 8 Marks):**
   - Constitutional History, Preamble, Fundamental Principles (250 Qs)
   - Fundamental Rights & Judicial Enforcement (Part III) (200 Qs)
   - Organs of State: Executive, Legislature, Judiciary (200 Qs)
   - Constitutional Bodies: Election Commission, PSC, CAG, ACC (150 Qs)
3. **Economy, Agriculture, Natural Resources & Development (1,200 Qs / 12 Marks):**
   - Macroeconomics: GDP, Budget, Inflation, Forex, Remittance, Five-Year Plans, Mega Projects (400 Qs)
   - Agriculture & Forestry: Crop varieties (BRRI/BARI), fisheries, livestock, tea (300 Qs)
   - Demography & Social Structure: Census data, indigenous communities, human development (250 Qs)
   - Industry, Commerce & Trade: RMG, export earnings, special economic zones (250 Qs)

1. **Computer Hardware & Architecture (450 Qs / 4.5 Marks):**
   - CPU, ALU, Registers, Cache, Motherboard, Bus architecture (150 Qs)
   - Input/Output Devices, Primary Memory (RAM/ROM), Secondary Storage (SSD/HDD) (150 Qs)
   - Number Systems: Binary, Octal, Hexadecimal, Boolean Algebra, Logic Gates (150 Qs)
2. **Operating Systems & Software Applications (450 Qs / 4.5 Marks):**
   - OS Principles: Process management, file systems, Windows, Linux, Android (150 Qs)
   - Application Software: Word processing, Spreadsheets, DBMS, SQL basics (150 Qs)
   - Programming Languages, Compilers, Interpreters, Algorithms (150 Qs)
3. **Computer Networks, Internet & Cyber Security (600 Qs / 6 Marks):**
   - Network Topologies, OSI & TCP/IP 7-Layer Models, Routers, Switches (200 Qs)
   - Internet Protocols: HTTP/HTTPS, FTP, DNS, DHCP, IP Addressing (IPv4/IPv6) (200 Qs)
   - Cyber Security: Cryptography, Malware, Firewalls, Cloud Computing, AI & IoT (200 Qs)

### SUB-07: Mathematical Reasoning (1,500 Questions)
1. **Arithmetic & Number Theory (500 Qs / 5 Marks):**
   - Real numbers, Divisibility, Prime numbers, GCD (গ.সা.গু) & LCM (ল.সা.গু) (150 Qs)
   - Percentage, Profit & Loss, Simple & Compound Interest (150 Qs)
   - Ratio, Proportion, Work & Time, Speed & Distance, Mixtures (200 Qs)
2. **Algebra & Functions (500 Qs / 5 Marks):**
   - Algebraic formulas, Factorization, Linear & Quadratic equations (200 Qs)
   - Inequalities (অসমতা), Absolute value, Logarithms, Exponents (সূচক ও লগারিদম) (200 Qs)
   - Arithmetic & Geometric Progressions (ধারা) (100 Qs)
3. **Geometry, Trigonometry & Coordinate Geometry (350 Qs / 3.5 Marks):**
   - Lines, Angles, Triangles, Quadrilaterals, Circles, Polygons, Pythagorean theorem (200 Qs)
   - Perimeter, Area, Volume of 2D and 3D shapes (100 Qs)
   - Trigonometric ratios & heights/distances (50 Qs)
4. **Permutation, Combination & Probability (150 Qs / 1.5 Marks):**
   - Fundamental counting principle, Factorials, Permutation ($nPr$) & Combination ($nCr$) (100 Qs)
   - Set theory (ভ্যানচিত্র) & Elementary probability (50 Qs)
